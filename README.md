### Text Hierarchy 
The text hierarchy for this dashboard was designed to facilitate rapid executive decision-making. By utilizing varied font weights, sizes, and color contrasts, the design establishes a clear "path of importance," moving from high-level KPIs to granular sub-category analysis.

#### Hierarchy Levels
- Level 1	Dashboard Title	Tableau Bold, 24pt, White	Immediate Context: High-contrast white text on a dark teal banner ensures the user instantly identifies the report's subject matter.
- Level 2	KPI Numbers (BANs)	Tableau Medium, 16pt, Bold	Core Metrics: These are the primary "Big Awesome Numbers." Large sizing allows for immediate scanning of 2018 year-end totals and YoY performance.
- Level 3	Section Headers	Tableau Medium, 14pt, White on Black	Categorization: Uses "inverse" formatting (light text on dark background) to create clear visual "anchors" for the different analytical sections.
- Level 4	Chart Labels & Axes	Arial, 10pt, Dark Grey	Data Support: Provides necessary detail for individual data points without cluttering the visual field or distracting from the primary trends.
- Level 5	Annotation/Footer	Arial, 9pt, Grey	Secondary Metadata: Contains data source, refresh dates, and helper text (logic definitions). Placed at the bottom to remain accessible but out of the primary focus.

### Color & Accessibility Logic
•	Semantic Color: Green is used exclusively for positive YoY growth (e.g., the 20.4% increase) to provide an instant "good/bad" indicator without reading text.
•	Hierarchy via Contrast: Darker backgrounds are used for titles to separate the "control" and "identity" areas from the "data" areas (white background charts).
•	Blue teal was used as a theme throughout the design, alongside white and black colors.
